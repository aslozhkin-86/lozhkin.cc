Digital wallet in a conservative country

m10 is a local digital wallet built for a relatively small market of Azerbaijan, with the challenge of speaking to two very different audiences at once: a more conservative adult users and Gen Z.

We deliberately avoided complex solutions. The interface relied on simple transitions, and we chose not to use effects like blur because a large share of users were still on older phones. Instead, we focused on creating delight through illustration, thoughtful flows, and an experience that felt clear and effortless. The result was a very simple product with a distinct personality, one that resonated with both audiences.

Digital card that gives you nothin but fun

We had strict business restrictions: no cashback, no special perks, and no big discounts. So instead of building the value around rewards, we focused on the experience itself: making the application flow smooth, the card design feel special, and the whole product genuinely enjoyable to use.
