aproach

Over the past few years, helping shape company culture has made me think more deeply about how we work together — and how much the environment around us shapes what we create. As a manager, I care about making room for people to do good work: building trust, keeping processes simple, and helping the team find a shared sense of direction. I want people to feel comfortable sharing an unfinished thought, asking for help, or questioning an idea. Those everyday moments are a big part of what makes a team work. That carries into how I design, too. I like to start at a whiteboard, where ideas are still rough and easy to change. There’s something inviting about working this way: everyone can add a thought, draw something, or help an idea take shape. It gives us a chance to build a shared understanding and a sense of ownership from the beginning. From there, I follow a simple rhythm: research, build, test, and keep learning. Along the way, I try to stay close to the core of the idea — what matters to people and what we’re helping them do. I find a lot of satisfaction in making that clear, letting the details support it, and knowing when to stop adding more.
